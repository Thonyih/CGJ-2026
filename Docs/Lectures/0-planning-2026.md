## What the images show

- **Page 2 · Calendar.** A pencil circles a day on a calendar, introducing the schedule that follows.
- **Page 4 · Tangram.** A square is cut into colored pieces on the left. The right-hand arrangement uses the pieces to form a different outline. For A2, the visual idea is to reuse piece geometry and change each piece’s position and orientation.
- **Page 5 · Pickagram.** The left sketch links a flat tangram layout to a vertical arrangement. On the right, two wireframe outlines and an arrow show a change in arrangement, beside a square subdivided into pieces. The drawing introduces the move from flat arrangements to a 3D composition.
- **Page 7 · Material samples.** A pair of test objects and a grid of shader balls show how surface appearance varies with color, highlight shape, reflection and detail. Reusing the same geometry makes the material differences easier to compare.
